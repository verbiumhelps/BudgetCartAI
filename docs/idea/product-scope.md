# BudgetCartAI - Product Scope Guardrails

## In-Scope (Foundational)
- Weekly budget setup and tracking
- Store-aware cart suggestions (substitutions, cheaper equivalents)
- Pantry inventory with expiry awareness
- Savings tracking (projected vs realized)
- Recipe suggestions constrained by pantry + budget (limited in free tier)

## Out-of-Scope (For Now)
- Social feeds, sharing, influencers
- Crypto rewards or gamified coupons
- Full grocery delivery fulfillment
- Generic AI chat assistant as primary UI
- International store coverage beyond initial SoCal rollout

## UI / Product Constraints
- Mobile-first
- Calm, trustworthy tone; subtle warmth
- AI is visible but not dominant (no chat-first experience)
- Only one primary optimization surfaced at a time
