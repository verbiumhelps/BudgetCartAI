# Growth + Monetization

## Free Tier
- Weekly budget tracking
- Basic spending analytics
- Manual pantry tracking
- Limited AI recipe suggestions (3/week)
- Limited store comparisons (3/week)

## Pro Tier (single tier)
Unlock:
- projected savings modeling
- unlimited substitutions + recommendations
- multi-store optimization
- discount alerts
- pantry automation
- waste forecasting
- unlimited recipes
- price volatility insights

## Implementation Boundary
This phase adds \src/monetization/entitlements.ts\ as a stable, deterministic entitlement map to wire into UI and paywall later.
